# Horizontmonstr

**Horizontmonstr** is a 1 lb combat robot that uses a **shuffling drive system** instead of wheels. Under many combat robot competition rules, shufflers receive a **50% weight bonus**, allowing the robot to carry a much heavier weapon than a standard wheeled robot.

The robot's horizontal spinner makes up **over 50% of the robot's total weight**, giving it the ability to deliver extremely powerful impacts despite competing in the same weight class.

I designed Horizontmonstr because many of the robots at my local competitions followed similar designs, and the fights often lacked the excitement I was looking for. By taking advantage of the shuffler weight bonus, I wanted to build something different—a robot with an exceptionally large weapon capable of producing exciting, high-energy matches for both competitors and spectators.

# Features

## 1. Shuffler Drive

Instead of conventional wheels, Horizontmonstr moves using two independently driven shuffling modules.

## 2. 50% Weight Bonus

Because it uses a shuffling drive, the robot qualifies for a 50% weight bonus under competition rules, allowing significantly more weapon mass than a conventional robot.

## 3. Massive Horizontal Spinner

The weapon accounts for over half of the robot's total weight, maximizing stored kinetic energy and impact power.

## 4. Compact Drive System

The robot is powered by two N20 gearmotors, providing a compact and reliable drive solution for the shuffling mechanism.

## 5. Belt-Driven Weapon

The weapon is driven using a timing belt, allowing the motor to be mounted independently while reducing shock loads transferred directly into the motor.

## 6. 4S Power System

Horizontmonstr runs on a 4S LiPo battery for increased weapon performance and faster spin-up.

# Assembly

Horizontmonstr is designed to be simple to assemble and maintain.

## Step 1 – Install the Electronics

Install all electronics according to the wiring diagram.

- Malenki Nano
- Weapon ESC
- Receiver
- Battery leads

---

## Step 2 – Mold the Silicone Feet

Cast the silicone feet using the printed molds.

Allow the silicone to fully cure before removing the feet from the molds.

---

## Step 3 – Assemble the Shuffler Modules

Press the silicone feet onto the cams.

Attach each cam to an N20 motor and secure the completed shuffler modules.

---

## Step 4 – Install the Drive Modules

Bolt both completed shuffler modules to the bottom plate.

Verify that both modules rotate freely without rubbing on the chassis.

---

## Step 5 – Install the Weapon System

Mount the weapon motor.

Press the motor pulley onto the motor shaft.

Attach the weapon pulley to the weapon.

Install the weapon shaft and bearings.

Fit the timing belt and adjust the belt tension.

---

## Step 6 – Assemble the Chassis

Attach the main body to the baseplate.

Install the battery and secure it in place.

Check that all wiring is clear of the weapon belt and moving components.

---

## Step 7 – Finish Assembly

Install the top plate and secure it with the screws.

The robot is now fully assembled and ready for testing.
