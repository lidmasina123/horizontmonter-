# horizontmonter-
Instead of wheels, Horizontmonstr moves using shuffling feet. This design earns a 50% weight bonus under competition rules, allowing a much larger weapon than other robots in the same weight class. The massive horizontal spinner makes up over 50% of the robot's total weight, delivering powerful impacts to opponents.

I designed Horizontmonstr because many of the robots at my local competition used similar designs and matches often lacked the excitement and energy I was looking for. By taking advantage of the shuffler weight bonus, I aimed to create a unique robot with an exceptionally large weapon that would make fights more exciting, dynamic, and memorable for both competitors and spectators.
